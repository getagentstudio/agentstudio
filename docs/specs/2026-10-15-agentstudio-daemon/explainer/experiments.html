What the experiments must decide

There is no demonstrated QUIC/codec winner, Rust-speed improvement, native echo percentile or complete Linux fork parity. The exact protocol, workload tolerances and supported platform profiles remain design work.

The six discriminators

Can a fresh real terminal restore at a declared cut and remain correct when following bytes arrive? Can sessions, authorized input and selected facts continue without views, while uncertain effects stay honest? Can one stalled viewer recover without corrupting others or retaining unbounded output? Can safe Linux realizations preserve the complete Mac capability contract, including refusals and residue? What does the mandated route cost under competing terminal, control and finite-content traffic? Can the iOS Gateway start safely, serve its UX socket and reconcile actual mobile lifecycle changes?
