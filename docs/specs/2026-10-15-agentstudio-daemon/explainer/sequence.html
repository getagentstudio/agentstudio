Eleven designs, one system

All eleven folders contain a scope brief and draft specification. Program Designs have not been completed. The independently accepted DS02 data-contract changes close only their bounded question; they do not make DS02 or the system ready for planning.

The dependency map

**DS01** identities, targeting, outcomes and schemas → **DS02** data meaning/units → **DS03** central authority and leases. **DS04** terminal runtime and **DS05** Git/discovery consume the shared contracts. They establish the behavior downstream domains depend on. **DS06** worktree fork; **DS07** agents/context; **DS08** files/review/output. Agents/context combines terminal and Git inputs; fork and review consume Git. **DS09** composes public entry, attached-machine CLI operations, multiple Hosts and adapters from the capability contracts. **DS10** realizes native publication, terminal interaction and declared client effects. Arrangement meaning comes from DS02. **DS11** composes the crate/owner DAG, installation, safety, pairing, shutdown, privacy and real whole-route proof.
