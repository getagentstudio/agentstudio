## Counterexamples that prevent false closure

- **Frame-valid but undeliverable:** an otherwise valid response larger than `4 MiB` but within the `16 MiB` response-frame ceiling can encode successfully and then overload an empty current connection writer. This is a source-derived branch, not an executed defect finding. A frame-size pass cannot certify delivery, total retained memory or application completion. N1/N3.
- **Decode-valid but contract-invalid:** current catalog tests mutate original bytes with an extra outer/metadata field, an invalid declared example or foreign catalog identity and expect rejection. Plain provisional Codable decode could discard unknown fields; the real catalog decoder validates original shape and identity. These test bodies establish intended discriminators, not a passing run. N2.
- **Lint-clean declaration but incomplete safety claim:** all workspace manifests inheriting forbid does not inspect third-party unsafe/FFI or prove an unbuilt generated bridge passes. Likewise a cancel token firing before shutdown join is not completion. N6/N8.
