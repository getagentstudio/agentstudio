## Cross-family facts and concrete counterexamples

- D **lock effect followed by error:** lock/unlock invokes native mutation before snapshot reread. A reread failure does not undo its marker effect; “threw means unchanged” is false. [Writer:193][lock-writer].
- D **aggregate is not atomic:** completeStatus computes facts and full-worktree line detail sequentially. A concurrent file/ref change can separate them; scoped facts do not scope the independent line count. [Status:40][status].
- D **returned removal is not unconditional removal success:** result contains separately observed directory/admin effects and optional prune/observation/incomplete failure. The result ID alone cannot establish removal. [Writer:152][remove-writer].
- D/R **staged success boundary is later than fetch:** private fetch precedes ref enumeration and guard construction; failures there can leave work. R native promotion's indeterminate commit and later-page cleanup error are source-backed counterexamples in the desk evidence, not new executed failures. [Stage:76][staging], [desk:83–86][remote-desk].
- D **deleted is not fully cleaned:** branch result can be deleted while config/reflog is leftInPlace due to recreation/reservation/removal failure. Its typed cleanup and lock residue cannot be flattened to Boolean. [Deletion types][delete-types].
- R **SDK query versus app fetching preview:** ordinary SDK reads are separate from CLI list/remove dry-run/prune preview, which can fetch before read/plan. App archive/branch/evidence policy and `.notChecked` activity do not arise from native remove. [Fork worksheet:61–70][app-policy] is the existing source-qualified policy inventory; no new native remove/prune contract is inferred.
