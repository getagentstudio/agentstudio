## Native and load-sensitive evidence

The [current proof model](../../../../../architecture/observability/observability_and_traceability.md:172) distinguishes marker/PID handoff from actual Victoria observations. It requires current instance/workload identity, allowlisted telemetry and separate proof layers. A stale state file or JSONL is not measured native performance. Source timestamps for synchronous MainActor execution and async owner roundtrip must not be conflated.

The [real-load requirement](../../../../../architecture/observability/observability_and_traceability.md:203) says small synthetic fixtures can miss MainActor pressure: interaction/MainActor claims need real-sized state or a stress fixture derived from a live heavy-load baseline. E3's finite semantic corpus is therefore a correctness discriminator and local cost profile, not sufficient evidence of acceptable loaded UI performance.

The [strict sidebar workload description](../../../../../architecture/observability/observability_and_traceability.md:371) has a different population from a terminal-fleet study: full watched roots, five tabs, twenty pane models and zero debug-owned PTYs in its idle/action populations. Its generation-matched semantic/accepted/native fingerprints and process-bound CPU sampler are documented obligations. They were not executed or independently source-traced through the workload script in this review. Do not add a PTY fleet to that profile and call it the same baseline. Terminal contention needs its own explicitly identified population.

## Consequence for E5

Keep four observations separate: fresh CLI call total, persistent client/socket exchange, owner application/effect and native terminal/render observation. Attribute stages with local monotonic durations and correlated identities, and measure end-to-end on the observing client clock. Current CLI measurement cannot price an extra Gateway hop by subtraction unless workloads, candidate identity and conditions are comparable; even then the comparison is aggregate rather than causal stage attribution.

The existing [E5 proposal](../../../experiments/primary-experiments.md#e5--full-route-latency-contention-and-compression) already separates fresh-process and persistent-client baselines and parser/pixel/presentation claims. Before any actual run, choose the exact claim and its independent observer, freeze the population and candidate provenance, and calibrate the instrument with a known perturbation. Product targets remain unselected; exploratory costs can still be reported honestly without a product pass verdict.
