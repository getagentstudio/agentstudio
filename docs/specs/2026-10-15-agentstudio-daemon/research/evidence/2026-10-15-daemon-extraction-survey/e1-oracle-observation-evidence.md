## Independence, coverage and unresolved observation limits

- **Common-defect risk:** Z/A formatter and continuation-validator/tracker files are identical. Agreement between two parses/serializations or applying the same OSC133 transform to reference and candidate can hide shared omissions/defects. Checked-in snapshot goldens, literal formatter expectations, direct semantic fields and committed-effect callback counts provide different checks, but still prove only the named corpus/field. A CRC/hash proves matching encoded bytes, not independent VT correctness or native presentation.
- **Profile boundary:** no source evidence mandates equality of every hidden bit. Required cells/styles, selected latent behavior, screens/modes/metadata and explicit normalization must be declared by the Lead/owner. Existing suppression of synchronized output is concrete normalization; missing tab stops/palette/inactive state cannot be silently called equivalent. Unobservable required elements are inconclusive, rather than guessed from text.
- **Suffix boundary:** the existing every-cut corpus demonstrates meaningful fixtures and chunking variants. It does not prove an arbitrary one-byte shift, default colored text or DSR query discriminates the chosen defect. A suffix that returns parser to ground may erase a difference; matching CPR may leave styles/text untouched. No omitted-residue/wrong-style/wrong-screen negative-control experiment ran here, and no particular new corpus/profile is selected.
- **Native boundary:** standalone VT APIs and internal Zig tests are not an already available app IPC/native surface inspection seam. Read_text may support text at a declared state checkpoint, subject to its actual selection and locking cost; styles/latent modes/residue/other screen and frame presentation need their actual qualified observation. Screen capture, query interception, sampling or a new hook was not performed or authorized by this report.
- **Pin boundary:** selected old/new test bodies were compared byte-for-byte: snapshot complete/history/alternate, canonical continuation, every-cut continuation, stream every-byte future behavior, pending-wrap save/restore, tabClear and mode1049 bodies match. DECRQM bodies differ, and Terminal/Screen/Parser/Stream/C ABI files have other changes. This does **not** establish cross-pin semantic equivalence; it avoids treating a common implementation as two independent references.
- **Exact remaining source gap:** candidate Capture's handler/row selection is inherited from the sibling backend evidence, not independently reopened in this lane. No other latest-upstream claim is made. Lead verifies that path and chooses the finite restoration profile/procedure; eight units above choose no target architecture or production mechanism.

Static verification only: eight O1–O8 rows, document under150lines, local link paths/line bounds checked. No test was executed and no runtime/pixel/query/calibration result is claimed.
